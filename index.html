Research

sentinelMAS: Evidence-Guided AI for Histopathology Classification

May 2026 – Present

AI Research Intern, University of Galway · Supervisor: Dr. Saeed Alsamhi

Designed an adaptive cascade in which a calibrated EfficientNet-B0 router uses conformal prediction to accept confident cases and escalate only uncertain ones to TinyViT and a frozen UNI foundation model, fused by a learned modality gate. Trained end to end on a single 16 GB T4 GPU, for settings with limited training data and compute.

The system reached 88.77% accuracy on the patient-disjoint CRC-VAL-HE-7K cohort, versus 78.59% for a standalone CNN and 88.22% for a brute-force ensemble, while cutting inference latency by about 35% relative to that ensemble. Leave-one-component-out ablations, blur and stain corruption tests, and multi-method explainability (Grad-CAM++, attention rollout, SHAP), audited with perturbation-curve faithfulness metrics, were used to check branch contributions and robustness under distribution shift.

Submitted to Cureus Journals (Springer Nature), under review.

Robustness of Self-Supervised ECG Representations to Graded Acquisition Noise

May 2026 – Present

AI Research Intern, Haqila Lab, Jimma University · Advisor: Dr. Hika Barki (Pukyong National University)

Built a paired, graded-corruption benchmark on PTB-XL comparing four self-supervised recipes (SimCLR, CLOCS, MAE, I-JEPA) and a supervised reference on a shared ViT-S, each pretrained from scratch with 3 seeds and evaluated by frozen linear probe. The benchmark covers 162 deterministic corruption conditions—real NSTDB artefacts, synthetic powerline, electrode-pop, and inverter noise, and mixtures—from −6 to 24 dB SNR, scored on AUROC, calibration (ECE), fixed-threshold F1, and CKA / effective-rank shifts.

AUROC alone hides failures: under −6 dB electrode motion, MAE and I-JEPA kept AUROC near 0.80 while NORM-class F1 collapsed to ≤ 0.004, and ECE rose from under 0.02 to 0.16–0.17 for SimCLR and MAE. Representation-geometry shifts track noise severity but do not reliably rank encoders, and seed variance can exceed between-method differences.

Accepted at PanAfriCon AI 2026, to appear in Springer CCIS.
