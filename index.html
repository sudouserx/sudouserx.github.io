Research

sentinelMAS: Evidence-Guided Multi-Agent AI for Histopathology Classification

May 2026 – Present

AI Research Intern, University of Galway · Supervisor: Dr. Saeed Alsamhi

Designed an evidence-guided adaptive cascade combining EfficientNet-B0, TinyViT, and the frozen UNI pathology foundation model, with calibrated confidence, conformal uncertainty, and learned modality gating for selective escalation.

The system reached 88.77% external accuracy on the patient-disjoint CRC-VAL-HE-7K cohort, compared with 78.59% for a standalone CNN and 88.22% for brute-force ensembling, while reducing total cohort inference latency by about 35%. Component ablations, corruption tests, and explainability analyses (Grad-CAM++, attention rollout, SHAP) were used to identify branch contributions and robustness under distribution shift.

Steering the Machine: Latent State Manipulation in Gemma-3-12B

August 2026

Independent research · Digital Minds Research Sprint, Apart Research

Investigated whether latent affective steering produces controlled semantic changes or broader behavioral degradation by intervening in late-layer residual streams (Layers 32 and 40) of Gemma-3-12B. Steering vectors were extracted and injected at strengths α ∈ {0.0, 0.05, 0.1} against magnitude-matched random-vector controls, and evaluated on self-reported valence, refusal, sentiment, and GSM8K reasoning under a 256-token generation constraint.

Affective steering produced semantic shifts, while high-magnitude random perturbations caused token looping and structural collapse. Refusal-oriented steering frequently produced hyper-rationalization rather than explicit refusal. I owned hypothesis design, experiment implementation, representation-engineering code, quantitative analysis, and manuscript preparation.
