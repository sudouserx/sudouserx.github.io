Research

sentinelMAS: Evidence-Guided Multi-Agent AI for Histopathology Classification

May 2026 – Present

AI Research Intern, University of Galway · Supervisor: Dr. Saeed Alsamhi

Designed an evidence-guided adaptive cascade combining EfficientNet-B0, TinyViT, and the frozen UNI pathology foundation model, with calibrated confidence, conformal uncertainty, and learned modality gating for selective escalation—an architecture built specifically to work under limited training data and compute.

The system reached 88.77% external accuracy on the patient-disjoint CRC-VAL-HE-7K cohort, compared with 78.59% for a standalone CNN and 88.22% for brute-force ensembling, while reducing total cohort inference latency by about 35%. Component ablations, corruption tests, and explainability analyses (Grad-CAM++, attention rollout, SHAP) were used to identify branch contributions and robustness under distribution shift.

Self-Supervised ECG Representation Learning Under Graded Noise

May 2026 – Present

AI Research Intern, Haqila Lab, Jimma University · Advisor: Dr. Gelan Ayana Zewdie

Benchmarking contrastive, reconstructive, and latent-predictive self-supervised methods for ECG representation learning under progressively graded physiological and environmental artifacts, including noise types specific to low-resource clinical settings (for example, inverter switching noise, 50 Hz powerline interference, and electrode pop). Steering the Machine: Latent State Manipulation in Gemma-3-12B

August 2026

Independent research · Digital Minds Research Sprint, Apart Research

Investigated whether latent affective steering produces controlled semantic change or broader behavioral degradation by intervening in late-layer residual streams (Layers 32 and 40) of Gemma-3-12B. Steering vectors were extracted and injected at strengths α ∈ {0.0, 0.05, 0.1} against magnitude-matched random-vector controls, and evaluated on self-reported valence, refusal behavior, sentiment, and GSM8K reasoning under a 256-token generation constraint.

Affective steering produced targeted semantic shifts, while high-magnitude random perturbations caused token looping and structural collapse. Refusal-oriented steering frequently produced hyper-rationalization rather than explicit refusal. I owned hypothesis design, experiment implementation, representation-engineering code, quantitative analysis, and manuscript preparation.

Founded Gobez after learning that only 3.4% of Ethiopian Grade 12 students passed the national university-entrance exam in 2024, despite a crowded market of existing “AI” study tools—and identified that those tools failed because they surfaced raw performance data instead of actionable, natural-language guidance.
